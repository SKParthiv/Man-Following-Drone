 # Project Requirements

 - Follow a Man (phone) from a distance of 1 mtr or 2 mtr.
 - Turn on cmd can be sent from the phone, while the phone records the exact location of itself to send to the drone.
 - The drone should be able to reach the Man from anywhere.
 - The environment is Dynamic and therefore the drone needs to be able to detect the surroundings and use pathfinding in case of path being blocked by a obstacle.
 - The drone should be lightweight and have a max speed of faster than a person's gait speed.
 - The drone should be able to run for a long duration. Preferably more than 6 hours.
 - Use of phone Navigation system to obtain the precise location and record the path taken in background.
 - The means of communication between phone and the drone on Power ON needs to long range.
 - The means of Communication between phone and drone at a range of 1m or 2m needs to have a high Data Tranfer Rate.
